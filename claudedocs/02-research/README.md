# Research Documentation

Technology evaluations, comparative analyses, and investigations.

## Purpose

This folder contains research findings, technology evaluations, and investigation results that inform project decisions.

## Structure

- `active/` - Currently ongoing research
- `archive/` - Completed research (moved here when concluded)
- `synthesis/` - Synthesized findings from multiple sources

## Contents

Place research documents here:

- **Technology Evaluations** - Framework/library comparisons
- **Best Practices Research** - Industry standards and patterns
- **Competitive Analysis** - Similar products/solutions
- **Feasibility Studies** - Technical feasibility assessments
- **Performance Research** - Benchmarks and optimizations

## Research Process

### Investigation Workflow
```
1. Identify Problem/Question
   ↓
2. Research Existing Solutions
   ↓
3. Conduct Technical Investigation
   ↓
4. Document Findings
   ↓
5. Create Implementation Guide
   ↓
6. Archive Research
```

### Documentation Standards
Research documentation should include:
- **Problem Statement** - What question or issue is being investigated
- **Methodology** - How the research was conducted
- **Findings** - What was discovered
- **Recommendations** - Suggested implementation approaches
- **References** - External resources and citations

## Guidelines

- Include sources and references
- Date all research (technology changes quickly)
- Mark obsolete research clearly
- Move completed research to `archive/`
