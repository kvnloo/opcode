## Status Indicators

- ✅ **Complete** - All requirements met
- 🔄 **In Progress** - Active development
- ⚠️ **At Risk** - Behind schedule or blocked
- ⏳ **Not Started** - Scheduled for future

| Pattern | Usage | Example |
