# Vision Documentation

Strategic vision and long-term product direction.

## Purpose

This folder contains high-level vision documents, product direction, and long-term strategic planning.

## Contents

Place vision documents here:

- **Product Vision** - Overall product direction and goals
- **Roadmap Narratives** - Story-driven roadmap explanations
- **Future Concepts** - Exploratory ideas for future versions
- **Market Positioning** - How the product fits in the market
- **User Personas** - Target user profiles and needs

## Subdirectories

- `concepts/` - Exploratory and conceptual designs

## Guidelines

- Focus on the "why" and "where we're going"
- Keep vision documents high-level
- Update periodically as strategy evolves
- Link to supporting research in `02-research/`
